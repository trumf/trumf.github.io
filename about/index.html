About

Who I am

I'm a design leader with a background in cognitive science. I build and lead design teams, and I design products that align with how people actually behave.

I have a strong foundation in user research and human factors, and I've established design practice inside agile product organisations. My industry experience spans automotive, fintech, and music — most recently as Head of Product Design at Helvo.

On this site I write about design leadership, the tools I build for my own craft, and how design connects to customer experience and brand.
